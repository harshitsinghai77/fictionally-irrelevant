## The Habit of Journaling

I recently read that private journaling is one of the most important habits you can cultivate. It clears your head, helps you process emotions, and often sparks creative ideas.  

I first learned about this through *morning pages* in Julia Cameron’s *The Artist’s Way*. The practice is simple: write three pages every day, without editing or censoring yourself. It’s a daily brain dump — a way to get unstuck, lighten your mood, and make space for clearer thinking.

[**Try Stillword Live**](https://stillword-app.netlify.app/)

---

## Discovering 750 Words

When I wanted to make journaling a daily habit, I found [750words.com](https://750words.com). It’s a neat well-designed site built to help you write every day. You get a private, password-protected space to write, with streak tracking, stats, and badges to keep you motivated.  

But after the 30-day free trial, it asked for a subscription:  
- **Standard Membership**: $5/month  
- **Fancy Membership**: $10/month with extra features  

For many people, that’s fair. But I couldn’t help thinking: *with today’s AI, couldn’t I just build this myself?*

---

## Why I Built Stillword

With AI and serverless cloud platforms, creating simple SaaS-style apps has become surprisingly easy. Instead of paying a subscription, I decided to spend a weekend building my own free alternative.  

I called it **Stillword** — a minimal daily writing app focused on consistency, not polish. It runs entirely on **serverless AWS**, which means I don’t pay for servers sitting idle. I only pay for actual usage, which is far cheaper (almost nothing) than a monthly subscription.  

And the best part? It only took me about **two hours** to design the UI, set up the backend, connect AWS services, test everything end-to-end, and deploy it live.

👉 [Try it here](https://stillword-app.netlify.app)

---

## What Stillword Offers

- **Distraction-free editor** with optional 750-word goal tracking  
- **Streak tracking & calendar** to visualize your writing history  
- **Themes & Zen mode** for a calming experience  
- **Guest mode** (no account needed) or **cloud sync** for entries across devices  
- **Export options** so your writing is always yours  

It’s not about publishing or sharing — it’s about writing for yourself, every day.

## AI makes it possible 

Stillword isn’t just about journaling. It’s also proof that with a bit of Software background + AI, you can build your own tools instead of paying for subscriptions.  

For simple, focused apps, serverless + AI = DIY SaaS. You don’t need 2 weeks of work. Sometimes, a few hours is enough to ship something useful, personal, and free.
